# 🚀 TrendPilot AI

TrendPilot AI is an advanced AI-powered content automation platform designed to identify trending YouTube topics, generate AI video prompts, create short-form videos automatically, and publish them directly to YouTube without manual intervention.

The system combines trend analysis, AI-generated content workflows, automated video production, and scheduled publishing into a fully automated content pipeline optimized for scalable short-form video creation.

---

# ⚡ Core Workflow

TrendPilot AI operates through a 3-stage automation pipeline:

## 📈 Trend Analysis Engine
Analyzes trending YouTube videos and extracts high-performing content patterns, viral topics, and engagement-focused ideas.

## 🤖 AI Video Generation System
Automatically generates optimized prompts for AI video creation tools and produces short-form AI-generated videos.

## 📤 Automated Publishing Engine
Uploads generated videos directly to YouTube channels with automated publishing workflows and scheduled content delivery.

The platform is configured to generate and upload multiple videos daily with minimal human involvement.
